## Research Interests

- **Factor Analysis and Dimension Reduction:** generalized factor models, sparse factor analysis, and sufficient dimension reduction.
- **Model Selection and Cross-Validation:** factor number selection, data thinning, and data-driven selection criteria for high-dimensional models.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Statistical Machine Learning:** transfer learning, representation learning, and statistical modeling to understand and explain phenomena in artificial intelligence.
- **Text Analysis and Complex Data:** statistical methods for text, count data, and high-dimensional heterogeneous data.
- **Biostatistics:** statistical modeling and analysis of biological and biomedical data.
- **Interdisciplinary Applications:** applications of statistics and machine learning in economics, environmental science, and social sciences.
